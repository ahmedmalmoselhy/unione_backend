# UniOne
### The All-in-One University Management Platform

**Manage your entire university — students, staff, academics, and administration — from a single, unified system.**

## Why UniOne?

Running a university means coordinating hundreds of departments, thousands of students, dozens of academic programs, and a large body of administrative and teaching staff — all at once. UniOne brings every piece of that puzzle into one place, giving each person in your institution exactly the tools they need, and nothing they don't.

No more juggling spreadsheets for grade entry, separate systems for enrollment, and email chains for announcements. UniOne is a purpose-built university platform with a polished administrative dashboard and a mobile-ready API for students and professors.

---

## What UniOne Does

### A Command Center for Leadership

University administrators get a real-time overview of the entire institution the moment they log in — total students, professors, employees, courses, and sections, broken down by faculty with interactive charts. A built-in **data health monitor** surfaces issues automatically: departments without heads, sections without assigned professors, students without department placements — before they become problems.

### Academic Structure, Fully Managed

UniOne models your university exactly as it is:

- **Faculties** with their own deans, logos, and enrollment rules
- **Departments** — both academic and managerial — with department heads and administrators
- **Courses** with credit hours, lab hours, prerequisites, and multi-department ownership
- **Sections** — specific course offerings tied to a term, a classroom, a schedule, and a professor
- **Academic Terms** with a full calendar: registration window, withdrawal deadline, exam period, and grade submission deadline — all tracked and enforced automatically

### Student Lifecycle, End to End

From the day a student is admitted to the day they graduate, UniOne tracks every step:

- Enrollment status (active, suspended, graduated, withdrawn)
- Department assignment and **department transfer history** with full audit trail
- Course enrollments per term, with capacity enforcement and registration window validation
- GPA — calculated automatically every time a grade changes
- A complete grade breakdown: midterm, final, coursework, letter grade, and grade points

### Staff Management

Professors and employees are first-class citizens in UniOne. Staff records capture employment details, academic rank, specializations, and office locations. Professors are assigned to sections, and their grading activity is reflected in real time for administrative oversight.

### Grading With Confidence

Grade entry happens through the professor-facing portal and syncs immediately to the administrative dashboard. Each grade records who submitted it, when — and every change is logged. Students are notified the moment their grade is posted.

### Announcements That Reach the Right People

Create announcements scoped precisely to who needs to see them — the whole university, a specific faculty, a single department, or one section. Faculty and department administrators can only see and manage announcements within their own scope, keeping communication clean and secure.

## A Role for Every Person

UniOne uses a layered permission system so each person sees exactly what they need:

| Role | What They Can Do |
|---|---|
| **System Admin** | Full access to everything — faculties, terms, audit logs, university-wide data |
| **University Admin** | Manage university identity, contact details, president, and vice presidents |
| **Faculty Admin** | Oversee all departments, staff, students, courses, and announcements within their faculty |
| **Department Admin** | Manage professors, employees, students, and courses within their department |
| **Employee** | View-only access to the dashboard for their area |
| **Professor** | Submit and update grades for their sections via the mobile/web portal |
| **Student** | Browse and enroll in courses, view grades, and receive notifications via the portal |

Administrators are automatically scoped — a faculty admin can only see their faculty's data, and a department admin only sees their department. There is no risk of cross-institutional data exposure.

## Student & Professor Portal (API)

Beyond the admin dashboard, UniOne ships with a full REST API designed for student and professor apps:

- **Students** can view their profile and academic record, browse available sections, enroll in courses, drop enrollments, and receive real-time notifications when grades are posted
- **Professors** can view their assigned sections and submit or update grades for any enrolled student
- **Everyone** gets a notification inbox — in-app and by email — keeping them informed without requiring a dashboard login

---

## Built-In Data Operations

UniOne makes bulk data management painless:

- **Import** professors, students, and grades from Excel — with downloadable blank templates to guide the process
- **Export** professors, students, employees, grades, and enrollments to Excel at any time
- All imports are validated before saving, with clear error feedback

---

## Complete Accountability

Every meaningful action in the system — creating a record, updating it, assigning a role, revoking one, transferring a student — is recorded in an immutable **Audit Log** with a before-and-after snapshot, the name of the person who performed it, and their IP address. System administrators can search, filter by action type, entity, and date, and trace any change back to its source.

---

## Bilingual by Design

UniOne is built for Arabic-speaking institutions. Every major entity — the university, faculties, departments, courses, academic terms — carries both an English name and an Arabic name. The platform is built to serve multilingual environments without compromise.

---

## Notifications That Keep Everyone in the Loop

UniOne sends the right notification to the right person at the right time:

- A student enrolls → they receive a confirmation notification
- A professor posts a grade → the student is notified immediately
- An admin is assigned or removed → they receive an email and an in-app notification
- A forced password change is required on first login — keeping credentials secure
